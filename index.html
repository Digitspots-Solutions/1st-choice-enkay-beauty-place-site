About Us

Poultry Farming Foods Technology Limited is a leading Nigerian FoodTech company dedicated to modernizing poultry farming through cutting-edge agricultural technology. Based in Bwari, we combine traditional farming expertise with innovative solutions to meet the growing demand for quality poultry products across Nigeria. Our commitment to food safety, sustainable farming practices, and technological innovation sets us apart in the industry. We work directly with farmers, distributors, and retailers to ensure the highest standards from production to consumption.

Our Services

Poultry Production & Management

Comprehensive poultry farming solutions utilizing advanced breeding techniques, automated feeding systems, and climate-controlled housing for optimal bird health and productivity.

Quality Poultry Products Supply

Fresh, farm-to-table poultry products including live birds, dressed chicken, eggs, and processed poultry items delivered across Nigeria with full traceability and quality assurance.

AgriTech Consulting

Expert guidance for farmers looking to modernize their operations. We provide technology integration, farm management systems, and sustainable farming practices consultation.

Feed Production & Nutrition

Scientifically formulated poultry feeds designed for different growth stages, ensuring optimal nutrition, faster growth rates, and better meat quality.

Cold Chain & Distribution

Reliable cold storage and distribution network ensuring your poultry products reach markets in perfect condition, maintaining freshness from our farms to your customers.
