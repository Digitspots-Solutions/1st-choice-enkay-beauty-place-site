About Us

1st Choice Enkay Beauty Place is your trusted partner for all your fashion and creative design needs in Bwari, Abuja. We specialize in creating stunning custom outfits that celebrate Nigerian culture while embracing modern trends. Our experienced designers understand the art of bringing your unique style to life, whether you need traditional native wear for special occasions, corporate attire, or contemporary fashion pieces. Located at 138 Byazhin Road, Byazhin Kubwa, we serve clients across Bwari and beyond with dedication to quality, creativity, and customer satisfaction. Every stitch, every fabric choice, every design detail is carefully considered to ensure you look and feel your absolute best.

Our Services

Custom Fashion Design & Tailoring

We create bespoke outfits tailored perfectly to your measurements and style preferences. From elegant aso-ebi to everyday wear, every piece is crafted with precision.

Traditional Native Wear

Celebrate your heritage with our stunning traditional Nigerian attire. We specialize in agbada, kaftan, iro and buba, and other cultural designs that honor Nigerian fashion traditions.

Contemporary Fashion

Stay on-trend with our modern fashion pieces that blend international styles with African flair. Perfect for the stylish Nigerian professional.

Bridal & Special Occasion Designs

Make your special day unforgettable with our exquisite bridal wear and occasion outfits. We handle everything from concept to final fitting.

Fabric Sourcing & Consultation

Not sure which fabric or style suits you best? Our expert team provides personalized consultation to help you make the perfect choice for any occasion.

Alterations & Repairs

Give your favorite outfits new life with our professional alteration and repair services.
